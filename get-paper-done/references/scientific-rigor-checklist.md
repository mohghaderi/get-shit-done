# Scientific Rigor Checklist

Use this checklist while planning and drafting scientific papers.

## Problem Framing

- Clear research question(s)
- Defined contribution and novelty
- Explicit scope boundaries

## Method Rigor

- Search protocol documented
- Inclusion and exclusion criteria specified
- Quality appraisal rubric applied
- Bias register maintained

## Evidence Quality

- Source quality score for each source
- Counterevidence captured for major claims
- Confidence level per major conclusion

## Reproducibility

- Exact search strings recorded
- Search dates and repositories recorded
- Repeatable screening and extraction process documented

## Ethics and Disclosure

- Ethical risks discussed
- Conflicts of interest disclosed
- Limitation statements explicit

## Publication Readiness

- Citation style consistent
- Figures licensed and attributed
- Claims traceable to source IDs
- Abstract, conclusion, and future work aligned with findings
